# Job Targeting

## Purpose

Job targeting is the stage where the RicFit AI Remote Job Workflow converts the player's career positioning into practical remote job categories.

The objective is to identify realistic opportunities that align with the player's actual experience, transferable skills, technical capabilities, career direction, and income requirements.

The workflow does not treat every remote job as equally appropriate.

## Targeting Principle

The system prioritizes roles where the player's existing experience and capabilities provide credible relevance.

The objective is:

**Target the right opportunities rather than simply increasing the number of applications.**

Primary and Secondary Targets

The workflow can distinguish between stronger and secondary career directions.

Primary Targets

Primary targets are roles where the player's existing experience and transferable skills provide the strongest alignment.

These should receive the greatest attention during the job search.

Secondary Targets

Secondary targets can expand the opportunity pool when they remain reasonably aligned with the player's capabilities.

Secondary targeting should not be used to justify applying for roles that require unsupported experience.

This helps determine whether a role should be considered a realistic target.

Sports-to-Role Translation

Sports experience can strengthen targeting when it demonstrates capabilities relevant to the target role.

For example:

Sports Experience
        ↓

        ↓

The system does not treat sports participation as equivalent to unrelated employment experience.

Instead, it identifies the professional capabilities that can legitimately transfer into the target role.

Application Quality

Job targeting supports the principle that application quality matters.

The system should prioritize opportunities where the candidate can present credible alignment.

A stronger targeting approach is:

Relevant Opportunity
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

rather than:

Any Remote Job
        ↓
Generic Application

Targeting Boundaries

The workflow should avoid targeting roles that require qualifications the player cannot support.

Examples include:

Unsupported professional licenses
Certifications the player does not hold
Technical expertise not demonstrated
Seniority beyond the player's evidence
Professional experience that does not exist
Specialized qualifications that have not been established

The purpose of targeting is not to make the player appear qualified for everything.

It is to identify the strongest realistic opportunities.

Human Review

AI can assist with identifying potential role categories and matching skills to requirements.

Human review remains responsible for determining whether the recommended direction is appropriate.

Human judgment is particularly important when:

Experience is ambiguous
A role requires specialized qualifications
Seniority is unclear
Transferable skills may be overstated
Compensation requirements limit available options
Additional information is needed

Design Objective

The purpose of job targeting is to create a focused and realistic job-search direction.

The system aims to help the player:

Understand which roles to pursue
Focus on relevant opportunities
Use transferable skills effectively
Avoid unrealistic targets
Align applications with career direction
Incorporate income objectives
Maintain consistency across platforms

The desired outcome is:

Better targeting before better applications.
